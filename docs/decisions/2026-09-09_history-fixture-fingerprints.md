# Exclude only classified immutable fixture findings

- Owner: `SIGNOFF-REPAIR.11.4.3.1.2.2`; REPAIR-0044.
- Evidence: docs/tasks/artifacts/signoff_review/history-fixture-fingerprints.md.

The two original pg_guard.rs findings are predictable 48-digit fixture strings,
not issued credentials. Historical/current source identity and transitive reader
inspection prove that their two synchronous tests only inspect local metadata;
they never connect. The separate live ownership proof is generated by the runner
and read from its environment. Classify from provenance and actual use, not merely
from a test filename or a plausible-looking value.

Use exactly the two immutable commit/file/rule/line fingerprints in .gitleaksignore.
Do not suppress the file, rule, arbitrary new values or all previous findings.
Native controls remove each entry independently and recover the corresponding
finding; an independent commit of identical content at identical file/line
positions still reports both findings. The actual root's default policy reports
zero. This is evidence of a narrow exception with retained detection, not a
repository-wide claim that secrets cannot exist.

On pinned Gitleaks 8.30.1, an explicit ignore path is additive: Detector also loads
the source-root .gitleaksignore. The initial empty-override omission control
therefore did not remove the real exceptions and correctly failed its expectation.
Preserve that result and use an isolated exact bare history without a working-tree
ignore file for omission controls. Do not weaken the real policy during testing or
mistake an alternate path for replacement semantics.

The fingerprint feature is version-sensitive. Requalify exact removal and future
commit detection when upgrading the scanner or changing exceptions. The full
history gate still runs before push and all other qualification gates remain.
